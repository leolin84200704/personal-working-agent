## Root cause
Bookings were made 7/15–7/21 while her weekly availability still included those windows; she then trimmed her weekly hours (after 7/21). Existing future events are NOT re-validated/flagged/rescheduled when availability shrinks, so they now sit outside her configured slots. Current engine (display + booking validation share ProviderAvailabilityService, same schedule + tz inputs) correctly excludes those times today.
