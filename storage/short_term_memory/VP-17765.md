# VP-17765 - Additional Notification Email Not Receiving Notifications

> Triage run 2026-08-18 (bug-triage skill). Ticket title is a misdiagnosis by the reporter;
> real complaint (Zendesk screenshot, accession 2606156897): "when I sign up for consults
> regarding Tests, I fill out my name, my email (suzanne@rootandreason.co) and my phone
> number and yet, the reminders always go to my business partner, Lori."

## Ticket Analysis

**Classification**: Class C/D (logic bug — calendar reminder recipient resolution), NOT Class A/B.
**Repos**: LIS-transformer-v2 (calendar). **Practice**: Root and Reason Functional Medicine,
provider/customer 40279 (Suzanne Baker), practice 144153. Clinical consults live in practice 150105.

## 診斷鏈 (evidence, all verified live 2026-08-18)

1. **Additional Notification Email 功能本身正常** — Postmark LIS server (8340335):
   suzanne@ appears in all 5 group notification emails (result_ready_new ×3,
   lab_shipped_patient_kit ×2, latest 2026-08-11), each per-recipient **Delivered**
   (gsmtp 250) **and Opened** by suzanne (07-08 even LinkClicked). Not in suppression
   list on either Postmark server (LIS + ZymeBalanz). Setting-consumer resolves
   contacts with contact_description `notification email` / `additional notification
   email_*` and comma-joins into Postmark To — working as designed.
2. **The asymmetry**: 21+ `calendar_prod` consult reminder emails ("See You in in
   48 hours/24 hours/15 minutes at Your Clinical Consult") went **only to
   lori@rootandreason.co**. v2_reminder_audit_log rows for events 12181/11787
   (Suzanne's Aug consults, accession 2606156897): 6/6 recipient_email=lori@, status sent.
3. **Root cause row**: calendar_prod.v2_calendar **35082** — calendar_owner_id 40279
   (Suzanne Baker), practice_id **150105**, role **patient**,
   calendar_owner_email = **lori@rootandreason.co** (created 2026-02-23). Her provider
   calendar 10113 (practice 144153) has contact@rootandreason.co. Every consult event's
   patient participant is calendar 35082 (22 events checked).
4. **Code mechanism** (LIS-transformer-v2 origin/main):
   - `reminder.service.ts` dispatch: recipient = each participant's
     `v2_calendar.calendar_owner_email` (filter at ~L172, send at L216/230).
   - Patient calendar for consults created by
     `provider-availability.service.ts#createPatientCalendarForCustomerIfAbsent` (L614),
     called from `meeting-request.resolver.ts#getLabClinicianAvailability` (L173,
     hardcoded practice 150105): **copies email from the owner's provider/clinicadmin
     calendar once** (findFirst WITHOUT practice filter), then `if exists → return`,
     never re-synced.
   - Booking-form email has no path in: `CreateEventByPatientInput` (event.model.ts:526)
     **has no email field**; meeting-request + public_booking confirmations also read
     `patientCalendar.calendar_owner_email`.
5. **Systemic blast radius**: of 15,389 patient-role calendars in practice 150105,
   **3,365 (~22%)** have calendar_owner_email different from the same owner's
   provider-calendar email (copy-once staleness; not all necessarily "wrong", but no
   sync mechanism exists).
6. No future events on calendar 35082 → no reminder pending right now.

## Retrospective / patterns worth promoting
- Ticket title ≠ symptom: PM framed as "Additional Notification Email" bug; attachment
  screenshot (Zendesk) contained the real, different symptom. Always pull the attachment.
- Postmark per-recipient MessageEvents (Delivered/Opened per address inside one
  multi-recipient send) cleanly separate "not included" from "not delivered" vs
  "delivered-but-denies" — decisive in <5 queries.
- calendar_prod recipient ground truth = v2_calendar.calendar_owner_email; it is a
  copy-once cache (created from provider calendar, never synced). New instance of the
  VP-9299 lesson: find the WRITER, not the reader — writer runs once at creation.
