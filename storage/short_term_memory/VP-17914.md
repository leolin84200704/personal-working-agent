## Verdict

- Not an EMR/LIS pipeline bug; nothing to repush (data never existed).
- Catalog fix already shipped (LBS-1723, 08-14). Residual impact: 13 pre-fix
  noGen orders / 13 received samples resulted without celiac; retroactive
  generation impossible without new specimens.
- 2608186060 answer: results CAN still be generated only after a new draw is
  received; the accession is otherwise correctly configured. Duplicate re-order
  2608176751 (same patient/panel) should probably be cancelled by the practice.
